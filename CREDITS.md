# Credits

None of these are included in this repo. The tools download from, link to, or work with them. Please support the people who made them.

## Bezels, TVs and CRT shaders
- **HyperspaceMadness**: Mega Bezel Reflection Shader for RetroArch.
- **Duimon**: Duimon Mega Bezel packs (the era TVs, monitors and handheld shells).
- **TheNamec**: Mega Bezel Commodore pack (the Commodore 1084S monitor used for the Amiga).
- **Sp00kyFox**: ScaleFX, used by the Amiga's smoothed look.
- **libretro**: the slang-shaders collection the wizard downloads Mega Bezel from.
- **guest(r)**: Guest Advanced CRT shader, used by most of the looks here.
- **Hyllian**: super-xBR.
- **MajorPainTheCactus**: Sony Megatron colour video monitor (the HDR option).

## Arcade cabinets
- **Mr. RetroLust**: MAME Lights Out Bezels Revisited.
- **OrionsAngel** and **estefan3112**: MAME realistic bezel artwork.
- **The Bezel Project**: arcade bezels (converted here into MAME artwork).

## Artwork, metadata and manuals
- **LaunchBox / Unbroken Software**: the LaunchBox games database and image server.
- **libretro**: libretro-thumbnails and libretro-database.
- **ArcadeDB** (adb.arcadeitalia.net): arcade flyers, screenshots, marquees and short gameplay videos.
- **ZXDB / Spectrum Computing**: the ZX Spectrum database, inlays and screens.
- **retrokit**: the manuals collection on archive.org.
- The **Nostalgic Platform Icons** and **Nostalgic Platform Clear Logos** LaunchBox media packs, which the menu-icon step reuses if you have them installed.

## 60 fps and widescreen settings (configs/emulators)
- **PCSX2** community patches (the patches bundled with PCSX2).
- **RPCS3** community patch database (patch.yml from rpcs3.net).
- **Xenia Canary** game-patches repository.
- **Dolphin**'s built-in game settings and their community Gecko codes.
