- Please don't ask for, or post links to, games or BIOS files in issues, discussions or pull requests. They will be deleted.
- The scripts fetch box art, screenshots and metadata only from public services that offer them for this purpose (the LaunchBox Games Database and image server, libretro-thumbnails and libretro-database, ArcadeDB, ZXDB / Spectrum Computing), and only for the games already in your own library.
- Bezel, TV and cabinet artwork belongs to its creators and is not included here. The wizard links to each official download page. Where a look needs a small variation of a pack's file (for example a Duimon preset with a different CRT shader), the wizard makes it on your own computer from your own copy, and it is never redistributed.
- The bezel preview pages are rendered on your own computer from your own games. No game screenshots are included in this repository.
